# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Fixed

- The audit log (`--audit-log`) and the review bundle's `compliance-checklist.md` no longer say "All automated checks passed" while a MAJOR or MINOR finding is open. When findings are open but none is a BLOCKER, the log shows a warning that gives the number of open findings by severity, for example "1 finding is still open (1 MAJOR) and no BLOCKER finding is open", and says that the draft still needs them resolved or reviewed and that substantive lawyer review is still required. The "All automated checks passed" note now appears only when every finding passes, the condition under which the text report says all required disclosures are present. The BLOCKER warning is unchanged. The playground wheel is rebuilt from the fixed source.
- DOCX drafts: a body paragraph with a bold lead-in, such as "**Note:** purchasers bear market, liquidity and technology risks.", no longer opens a risks section. Before, any paragraph under 120 characters with one bold run counted as a heading, so a draft with no risk section could pass the ANNEX_I.I blocker. A paragraph is now a heading only if it has a Heading style or an outline level, or is a short label (at most 12 words, no sentence punctuation) that is entirely bold or in capitals. A Title-style paragraph names the white paper and opens no section.
- PDF drafts: a short body line that mentions a section word, such as "risks", no longer opens that section. A line is now a heading only if it names a section, has at most 12 words (20 when numbered), does not end with sentence punctuation, and is either numbered ("1.", "5.2", "A.1", "I.", "Part I") with a capital after the number or written in title case or capitals. An unnumbered heading in sentence case, such as "Risk factors", is no longer recognised in a PDF.
- DOCX and PDF drafts: heading text no longer counts as section body. A section that holds only its heading is reported missing with 0 words.
- "TBD", "TBC", "[●]", "[•]", "to be confirmed" and "to be determined", in any case and with or without punctuation, are now reported missing when they are the whole content of a section or of a numbered field. Before, they counted as a one-word answer under review. A numbered field holding a placeholder is unanswered and does not produce the "marked not applicable" note. The notification date of `examples/incomplete.json` ("TBD.") is now missing, so the sample report, the demo capture and the README and DETAILS output blocks are regenerated. The playground wheel is rebuilt from the fixed source.
