# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Fixed

- The audit log (`--audit-log`) and the review bundle's `compliance-checklist.md` no longer say "All automated checks passed" while a MAJOR or MINOR finding is open. When findings are open but none is a BLOCKER, the log shows a warning that gives the number of open findings by severity, for example "1 finding is still open (1 MAJOR) and no BLOCKER finding is open", and says that the draft still needs them resolved or reviewed and that substantive lawyer review is still required. The "All automated checks passed" note now appears only when every finding passes, the condition under which the text report says all required disclosures are present. The BLOCKER warning is unchanged. The playground wheel is rebuilt from the fixed source.
- DOCX drafts: a body paragraph with a bold lead-in, such as "**Note:** purchasers bear market, liquidity and technology risks.", no longer opens a risks section. Before, any paragraph under 120 characters with one bold run counted as a heading, so a draft with no risk section could pass the ANNEX_I.I blocker. A paragraph is now a heading only if it has a Heading style or an outline level, set on it or on the style it is based on, or is a label of at most 20 words and fewer than 200 characters that is entirely bold or in capitals. So the part titles of the Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2984 template stay headings with or without the part letter: they run to 20 words, and without the letter Part E has 14, Part B 16 and the German Part A 13. A lone dash is not a word, a label does not start with a bullet or dash, and it has no sentence punctuation after a word; abbreviations such as "incl.", "bzgl." or "Corp." do not count as a sentence end. "Entirely bold" means every letter after a leading number, including the text of a hyperlink, so "5." in plain type before a bold "Risk Factors" still makes a heading. A Title-style paragraph names the white paper and opens no section. A heading that names no section, such as "Glossary", ends the current section instead of leaving its text to the section before, unless it is a sub-heading: a deeper outline level than the heading that opened the section, or a bold or capitals label under a heading that has a Heading style or an outline level. A numbered field of a template part ("A.1 Name") stays in its part. A style that is based on itself through a loop no longer hangs the loader, and a style based on a numbering (list) style no longer stops it with an error. Limits: a sentence of up to 20 words without end punctuation that is entirely bold or in capitals is read as a heading; a bold label that names no section, such as "Glossary", stays in a section opened by a Heading-style heading; table cells are not read, as before, so answers kept in a table count for no section, and a part whose answers are all in a table reads as missing (before, its title counted as its text).
- PDF drafts: a short body line that mentions a section word, such as "risks", no longer opens that section. Before, every line under 120 characters that named a section opened it. The text visitor of pypdf now gives the font size, weight and height of each line; on a page where its text runs do not add up to the page text, the lines are read without font information. A line is in a heading font if it is at least 15 % larger than the body text, or bold where the body text is not. A heading that wraps is joined into one line before the lines are read: consecutive lines in the same heading font are joined where the second sits one line spacing (at most 1.75 times its font size) below the first, and "crypto-" and "assets" make "crypto-assets". pypdf misreports the heights of lines laid out with the T* operator, so where the body lines seem more than 2.5 times their size apart, the font alone decides. A line without a heading font is joined with the next line where it runs on ("or", "the", "their", a comma or a hyphen), the next line starts in lower case, and the two make a label that names a section. A numbered line, a list item and a line on another page are never joined to the line before.
- PDF drafts: a line in a heading font that names a section is a heading if it is a label: at most 20 words, no sentence punctuation at the end, not a bullet item, and a capital after any numbering. This holds whatever the lines around it, so a heading followed by a list item in lower case ("a) Market risk") or ending with a colon is a heading. The exception is a numbered line right after a numbered body line that is also in a heading font (a bold numbered list), which ends the current section. A line without a heading font is a heading if it is shaped like one: at most 12 words (20 when numbered, a lone dash not counted), no sentence punctuation or colon at the end, and either in title case or capitals, or numbered as in the template ("Part I", "A.1", the summary fields "01" to "10") with a capital after the number. Unless it comes before the first heading, a line numbered "1.", "5.2", "I." or "A." must also be in title case or capitals and follow a heading or a line that ends a sentence or holds only a placeholder or "N/A". Where the draft sets its headings in a heading font (at least two lines in a heading font name a section), a line in the body font is a heading only if it is numbered as in the template, and a title-case line in the body font ends the current section.
- PDF drafts: without a heading font, bullet and dash items, numbered list items in sentence case, the line after a lead-in that ends with a colon, a line that a sentence runs into or out of (a lower-case list item such as "a)" after it does not count), and wrapped lines of a capitals paragraph are body text of the current section. Any other short line that names a section ends the current section, and the text under it is credited to no section: a heading in sentence case ("Risk factors"), a German heading with a lower-case adjective ("Beschreibung der wesentlichen Risiken"), a title-case line that ends with a colon, a line that ends with a connective such as "and" and is not joined to the next line, and a numbered heading after a bullet list or a table. That section is then reported missing instead of passing the check of the section before on its text. A heading that names no section also ends the current section: a line in a heading font that is not smaller than the heading that opened the section, and a template part title ("Part K"). A line in a smaller heading font is a sub-heading and stays in its section, and a numbered field of a part ("A.1 Name") stays in its part. The Part F title "Information about the crypto-assets" now opens the crypto-asset section; the plural in the titles of Parts E and G does not. A running page header or footer, that is a line among the first or last two of its page whose text, numbers aside, is also among the first or last two lines of another page, is body text of the current section and is skipped as the line before or after the lines around it.
- PDF drafts in the layout of the 2024/2984 summary table, where pypdf reads the field number, label and answer as one line ("01 Date of notification 2 February 2026"): a summary field that names no section, such as "02 Statement in accordance with Article 6(3)", ends the section of the summary field before it, so the statements of fields 02 to 07 no longer count as the notification date. The text after the label on a numbered field line is the answer of its section, so the notification date reads "2 February 2026". Fields 02 to 07 name no section, so their statements count for no section.
- Limits of the PDF heading rule: without font information, an unnumbered heading of more than 12 words is body text, so its text joins the section before, and a sentence-case heading ends the section, so its section reads as missing. In a draft with a heading font, a heading set in the body font that is not numbered as in the template ends the section, so its section reads as missing. Two headings in the same heading font one line spacing apart (a heading over an empty section with no space between the paragraphs), and in a PDF laid out with T* any consecutive lines in the same heading font, are read as one heading that opens the sections of both. A lead-in line in a heading font that names a section ("Holders should note these risks:") opens that section. A running header that is on one page only, or not among the first or last two lines of its page, is read like any other line. A heading that names several sections, such as the Part A title (the offeror and admission to trading), credits its text to each of them, as before.
- DOCX and PDF drafts: heading text no longer counts as section body. A section that holds only its heading is reported missing with 0 words. Required terms must now appear in the body text, so a PDF field "01 Date of notification" answered only "2 March 2026", on the next line or on the field line, leaves the notification date for review, because the word "date" sits in the heading. Regime inference reads the whole extracted text of a PDF or DOCX draft, headings and text in no section included, so a draft whose only ART or EMT marker is a heading such as "Reserve of Assets" or "Stabilisation Mechanism", or an introduction before the first heading, is read as that regime when no regime is stated.
- A section whose whole text is placeholders is now reported missing with 0 words and the issue "Section holds only placeholder text such as TBD or TBC.". Before, it counted as a thin answer under review. Placeholders are "TBD", "TBC" and "TBA" (also written "t.b.d."), "to be confirmed", "determined", "announced", "advised" or "provided", "[●]", "[•]", "[...]", "[…]", "[insert ...]", "XX" and longer runs of X, and in German drafts "noch offen", "folgt", "wird nachgereicht" and "N.N.", in any case and with any punctuation around them. In the numbered-field layout a field that holds a placeholder is unanswered: it contributes the placeholder instead of "Label: TBD", does not produce the "marked not applicable" note, and a placeholder in the language field is reported on the language check. A part with no answered field and at least one placeholder contributes only its placeholders, so it is reported missing even when its other fields say "N/A". Findings stay per section: a placeholder field in a part with an answered field, or a placeholder next to other text in a section, is not reported. The notification date of `examples/incomplete.json` ("TBD.") is now missing, so the sample report, the demo capture and the README and DETAILS output blocks are regenerated. The playground wheel is rebuilt from the fixed source.
- Study runs (`micar-lint-batch`): PDF and TXT sections use the shape rule of `micar-lint` without font information, so a body line that names a section no longer opens it. PDF sections are read line by line; before, the PDF text was joined into one line first, so a PDF yielded no sections. Study runs read no fonts or pages, so for a PDF whose headings are marked by a heading font they can differ from `micar-lint`: a sentence-case heading ("Information about the issuer") ends the section, an unnumbered heading of more than 12 words is body text, and a running page header is read like any other line.
