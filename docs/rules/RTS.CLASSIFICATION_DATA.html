How the status is set

  • Missing: in a numbered-field XHTML draft, every field that carries an item the Annex lists without “where available” is empty, absent or holds only a placeholder.
  • Review: one of those fields is empty, absent, holds only a placeholder or is marked not applicable: the type of white paper, the type of submission, the website, the starting date, the publication date, the languages, the personal data flag and the home and host Member States, the issuer's name, and its legal form and registered address unless its LEI field gives an LEI with valid check digits. In Table 2 the issuer is read from Part B where field B.1 is true (or, where B.1 says neither, where B.2 names one), otherwise from Part A. Also: the offer field gives ATTR and the market identifier code field is not answered (item 8); the DTI field is answered and the group DTI field is not (Art. 3(1)), or the other way round (Art. 3(2)); both give the same code; or neither gives a DTI, where Art. 3(3) asks for an identifier approved by ESMA, which the template has no field for. The finding names the field and the item, never the answer.
  • Not run: in a draft without field numbers (JSON, PDF, DOCX, Markdown, heading-based XHTML) the check does not run. The report has no finding for this rule, neither open nor passed; one note names how many field-level checks did not run.
  • Pass: none of the above. Where a field gives an LEI, the finding notes that Art. 2 also asks for its inclusion in the GLEIF database, which the linter does not check.

The forms of these fields (dates, LEI, DTI, MIC, true or false) are checked by ITS.FIELD_FORMATS and their codes (white paper type, submission type, Member States) by ITS.CODE_LISTS; COMMON.LANGUAGE looks for an official EU language. This rule does not report those defects again. The items of the Annex and the fields that carry them:

  • Reads the fields of a numbered-field XHTML draft by their numbers; text elsewhere is not considered. It does not run on a draft without field numbers; finding the items in free text is not built. Its finding is anchored to the section of the Part that carries the register data (crypto_asset, art or emt).
  • Checks whether each field holds an answer, not what it says or whether it is correct; it does not check a field's label, so a draft that numbers its fields otherwise is read in the wrong fields.
  • Art. 2 asks for each LEI to be valid and included in the GLEIF database. The check works offline and queries no registry: ITS.FIELD_FORMATS checks the LEI's form and ISO 17442 check digits, and the finding asks a reviewer to confirm that the LEI is listed and current.
  • Does not check a DTI's check character, its type (an identifier of type 3 for the group) or the DTI registry, and cannot tell whether an identifier given in free text is one ESMA approved under Art. 3(3).
  • Items 23, 24 and 26 are assigned by the competent authority and items 18 and 19 have no field, so they are not checked. Item 21 asks for the date and time of the latest notification in UTC; the template carries a date in field 01 (I.01), which ITS.NOTIFICATION_DATE_FORMAT checks, and Art. 1(3) lets the data be given in the white paper's own format. The legal form is not checked against the ISO 20275 list of entity legal forms.
  • The optional items (4 to 7, 9, 11, 12, 17, 27 and 28) are not required; the template's own conditions on fields such as F.7 (commercial name where no DTI is given) are checked by the point rules (ANNEX_I.F.P2, ANNEX_II.B.P2, ANNEX_III.B.P2), which also report a blank field of the register block under the Annex point.
  • A host Member State field marked not applicable goes to review, as the Annex lists item 15 without “where available”; where the crypto-asset is offered in no host Member State, a reviewer confirms that.
  • The English text gives item 20 (publication date) without a condition and item 17 “where applicable”; the German text reads “gegebenenfalls” and “sofern verfügbar”. The check follows the English text and the template.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • The checks of the Delegated Regulations (sustainability indicators, register classification data) have not yet been run against a real notified white paper.
