Date of notification written as YYYY-MM-DD, the template's format

How the status is set

  • Missing: in a numbered-field XHTML draft, field 01 of Table 2 (I.01 of Tables 3 and 4) is empty, absent or holds only a placeholder; in any other draft, section notification_date is absent or empty or holds only a placeholder.
  • Review: the field holds anything but one valid calendar date written YYYY-MM-DD, such as 02.03.2026, 2 March 2026, 2026-02-30, N/A or a date with words around it; in any other draft, the section holds no valid date written YYYY-MM-DD.
  • Pass: the field holds exactly such a date (quotation marks and a final full stop aside, and the template's field name where it runs into the answer), or the section of a draft without field numbers holds one anywhere. COMMON.NOTIFICATION_DATE checks that a date is given at all; this rule checks its form.

Limitations

  • Reads the fields of a numbered-field XHTML draft by their numbers; text elsewhere is not considered. In a draft without field numbers it reads section notification_date for a date.
  • Reads the text a reader sees. An Inline XBRL document can show a tagged value in another form than the value it reports, through a transformation; the check does not read the tags, so a reviewer confirms the tagged value of each field it reports.
  • Checks the form only. It does not confirm that the date is the date of notification.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • The template checks have not yet been run against a real notified white paper; for Tables 3 and 4 they follow the Official Journal text only.
