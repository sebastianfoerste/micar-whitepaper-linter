MARKETING.WHITE_PAPER_AND_CONTACT

States that a white paper has been published and gives a website, a telephone number and an e-mail address

How the status is set

  • Missing: the text is empty or holds only placeholders such as TBD.
  • Review: one or more of these is not found: a statement that a crypto-asset white paper has been published, a website address, a telephone number, an e-mail address.
  • Pass: all four are found.

Limitations

  • Reads the whole text of the marketing communication as a reader sees it: the text value of a JSON file, or the text of a Markdown, text, HTML, XHTML, DOCX or PDF file without what the loader finds a reader cannot see. It cannot see layout, placement, prominence, images, audio or video, so wording shown only in an image, a banner or a voice-over is not read.
  • Recognises a sentence that says a white paper has been published, in English or German, a web address (with a scheme, starting with www. or a host with a path), a telephone number and an e-mail address, as the contact checks of Annex I Part A point 6 recognise them. It cannot tell whose website, number or address they are, or whether they work.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • The marketing communication checks have not yet been run against a real marketing communication.
