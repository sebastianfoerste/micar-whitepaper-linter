How the status is set

  • Missing: section offeror is absent or empty, or holds only placeholder text such as TBD or TBC.
  • Review: the section has fewer than 50 words, or a listed term does not appear (case-insensitive substring match, with any run of spaces and line breaks read as one space and a word broken after its hyphen at a line end joined; a term shown with a pattern counts only where the pattern matches, so that a look-alike word does not stand in for it).
  • In a numbered-field XHTML draft, Review also follows when a field of the section holds only a placeholder such as TBD; the finding names the field by its number and, in English, the template's name.
  • Pass: none of the above. A pass means the check matched, nothing more.

Limitations

  • Reads one section key, offeror. Text elsewhere in the draft is not considered.
  • Matches words and counts length. It does not test accuracy, consistency with other sections or the point-level items of the cited Part.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
