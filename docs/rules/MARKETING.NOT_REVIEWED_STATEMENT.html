MARKETING.NOT_REVIEWED_STATEMENT

Statement that no competent authority has reviewed or approved the communication (wording only)

Citation
Art. 7(1), point (e) MiCAR
Canonical form
Art. 7 Abs. 1 Buchst. e MiCAR
Runs for
Marketing communications · crypto-assets other than ARTs and EMTs (Art. 7 MiCAR)
Severity if open
BLOCKER
Section key
marketing_communication
Minimum words
none: checks for the statement's wording
Listed terms (English)
this crypto-asset marketing communication has not been reviewed or approved by any competent authority in any member state of the european union, of the crypto-asset is solely responsible for the content of this crypto-asset marketing communication

diese kryptowert-marketingmitteilung wurde von keiner zuständigen behörde eines mitgliedstaats der europäischen union geprüft oder genehmigt, trägt die alleinige verantwortung für den inhalt dieser kryptowert-marketingmitteilung

How the status is set

  • Missing: the text is empty or holds only placeholders such as TBD.
  • Review: either sentence of the statement is not in the text word for word, in English for an English draft and in German for a German one, or the words before "of the crypto-asset is solely responsible" name neither the offeror nor, under the second subparagraph, the person seeking admission to trading or the operator of the trading platform.
  • Pass: the statement is there in that wording.

Legal basis

Art. 7(1), point (e) MiCAR

  • https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng

Checked on 2026-11-25 against the Official Journal text of Art. 7(1) MiCAR in English and German (OJ L 150, 9.6.2023; CELEX 32023R1114), as the Publications Office Cellar served it that day. Amendments and corrigenda of Art. 7 were not checked separately.

Limitations

  • Reads the whole text of the marketing communication as a reader sees it: the text value of a JSON file, or the text of a Markdown, text, HTML, XHTML, DOCX or PDF file without what the loader finds a reader cannot see. It cannot see layout, placement, prominence, images, audio or video, so wording shown only in an image, a banner or a voice-over is not read.
  • Matches the English and German wording of Art. 7(1)(e) MiCAR only. The draft's language decides which, so a statement in the other language or in a third one goes to review. Case, line ends and soft hyphens are ignored. It cannot see whether the statement is clear and prominent. The German second subparagraph names the “Person, die die Zulassung zum Handel beantragt” and the “Betreiber der Handelsplattform” without printing a sentence for them; the check accepts both with or without “des Kryptowerts”.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • The marketing communication checks have not yet been run against a real marketing communication.
