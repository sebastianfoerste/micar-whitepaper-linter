How the status is set

  • Missing: section summary is absent or empty, or holds only placeholder text such as TBD or TBC.
  • Review: the section has fewer than 80 words, or a listed term does not appear (case-insensitive substring match, with any run of spaces and line breaks read as one space).
  • The predefined warning of Art. 6(7), Art. 19(6) and Art. 51(6), second subparagraph, does not count toward the minimum. A numbered-field XHTML draft leaves out field 07 of Table 2 (I.04 of Table 3, I.05 of Table 4) with its label, and any draft leaves out the warning's sentences where they appear in the template's English or German wording. The finding then gives the words besides the warning.
  • Review also follows when a point of that warning, (a) to (d), is not found in the template's English or German wording, whichever language the draft is in; the finding names the points not found. The patterns also take the Regulation's own openings (“the summary should be read”, “the crypto-asset white paper does not constitute”) where the template says “this”. A numbered-field XHTML draft is read by field 07 of Table 2 (I.04 of Table 3, I.05 of Table 4) alone: a field that is empty, absent or holds only a placeholder such as TBD counts as absent, and the finding names it.
  • For an asset-referenced or e-money token, Review also follows when no sentence names redemption (“redemption”, “redeem”, “Rücktausch”) together with “at any time” or “at all times” (“jederzeit”), the statement of Art. 19(6) and Art. 51(6), third subparagraph, and for an e-money token when par value (“par value”, “at par”, “Nennwert”) is not named in the text read. A numbered-field XHTML draft is read by field I.06 of Table 3 or I.07 of Table 4 alone, with the same rule for an empty or placeholder field. Other crypto-assets are not asked for a redemption statement.
  • In a numbered-field XHTML draft, Review also follows when a field of the section holds only a placeholder such as TBD; the finding names the field by its number and, in English, the template's name.
  • Pass: none of the above. A pass means the check matched, nothing more.

Limitations

  • Reads one section key, summary. Text elsewhere in the draft is not considered.
  • Matches words and counts length. It does not test accuracy, consistency with other sections or the point-level items of the cited Part.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • Recognises the warning only in the template's English or German wording, with the Regulation's own openings. A warning in other words counts toward the 80 words and goes to review, as does a point split across sentences. The check cannot see whether the summary follows the statement of the management body, as the cited paragraphs require.
  • Reads the redemption statement from one sentence that names redemption and “at any time” (for an e-money token, par value anywhere in the text read). The conditions for redemption, which the same sentence of the Regulation requires the summary to state, are not checked, and nor is whether the statement is true. In a numbered-field XHTML draft a warning or redemption statement outside its template field is not seen.
