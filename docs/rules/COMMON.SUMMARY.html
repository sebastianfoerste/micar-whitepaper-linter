How the status is set

  • Missing: section summary is absent or empty, or holds only placeholder text such as TBD or TBC.
  • Review: the section has fewer than 80 words, or a listed term does not appear (case-insensitive substring match, with any run of spaces and line breaks read as one space).
  • The predefined warning of Art. 6(7), Art. 19(6) and Art. 51(6), second subparagraph, does not count toward the minimum. A numbered-field XHTML draft leaves out field 07 of Table 2 (I.04 of Table 3, I.05 of Table 4) with its label, and any draft leaves out the warning's sentences where they appear in the template's English or German wording. The finding then gives the words besides the warning.
  • In a numbered-field XHTML draft, Review also follows when a field of the section holds only a placeholder such as TBD; the finding names the field by its number and, in English, the template's name.
  • Pass: none of the above. A pass means the check matched, nothing more.

Limitations

  • Reads one section key, summary. Text elsewhere in the draft is not considered.
  • Matches words and counts length. It does not test accuracy, consistency with other sections or the point-level items of the cited Part.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • Only sentences in the template's English or German wording are left out. A warning in other words still counts toward the 80 words.
