How the status is set

  • Missing: section technology is absent or empty, or holds only placeholder text such as TBD or TBC.
  • Review: the section has fewer than 60 words, or a listed term does not appear (case-insensitive substring match, with any run of spaces and line breaks read as one space).
  • Pass: none of the above. A pass means the check matched, nothing more.

Limitations

  • Reads one section key, technology. Text elsewhere in the draft is not considered.
  • Matches words and counts length. It does not test accuracy, consistency with other sections or the point-level items of the cited Part.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • The Annex III rules have not yet been run against a real EMT white paper.
