RTS.SUSTAINABILITY_INDICATORS

Sustainability indicators S.1 to S.9, and S.10 to S.16 above 500 000 kWh, in the format of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/422

How the status is set

  • Missing: in a numbered-field XHTML draft, none of fields S.1 to S.9 holds an answer other than a placeholder.
  • Review: one of S.1 to S.9 is empty, absent, holds only a placeholder or is marked not applicable (Art. 4(1)); S.6 or S.7 is not one date written YYYY-MM-DD, or S.6 is later than S.7; S.8 is not one number in kWh of up to 18 digits with up to 5 decimals and a full stop as decimal separator ({DECIMAL-18/5}; “per year” may follow); S.8 exceeds 500 000 kWh and one of S.10 to S.16 is empty, absent, a placeholder or marked not applicable (Art. 4(2)); S.8 cannot be read and one of S.10 to S.16 is not answered; or an answered S.10 is not a percentage {DECIMAL-11/10} or an answered S.11 to S.14 is not an amount {DECIMAL-18/5}, with up to five words for its unit. An amount in MWh, GWh or TWh is converted for the threshold and still goes to review.
  • In a draft without field numbers (JSON, PDF, DOCX, Markdown, heading-based XHTML): Missing where section environmental_impact is absent, empty or holds only a placeholder, otherwise Review with a note that the field-level check needs the numbered-field layout. Such a draft never passes.
  • Pass: none of the above.

Limitations

  • Reads the fields of a numbered-field XHTML draft by their numbers; text elsewhere is not considered. In a draft without field numbers it reads section environmental_impact only to tell whether it is absent.
  • Reads the text a reader sees. An Inline XBRL document can show a tagged value in another form than the value it reports, through a transformation; the check does not read the tags, so a reviewer confirms the tagged value of each field it reports.
  • Checks S.1 to S.16 only. The optional indicators S.17 to S.36 of Table 4 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/422 and their sources are not checked, nor the content of any indicator, the calculation guidance of Art. 6 or whether S.1 to S.5 agree with the fields of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2984 they refer to.
  • S.8 of exactly 500 000 kWh does not exceed the threshold of Art. 4(2).
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • The checks of the Delegated Regulations (sustainability indicators, register classification data) have not yet been run against a real notified white paper.
