MARKETING.IDENTIFIABLE

Clearly identifiable as a marketing communication (a label in the title or the opening words)

How the status is set

  • Missing: the text is empty or holds only placeholders such as TBD.
  • Review: neither the title nor the first 40 words hold a label that marks the text as marketing.
  • Pass: the title or the opening holds such a label.

Legal basis

Art. 7(1), point (a) MiCAR

Limitations

  • Reads the whole text of the marketing communication as a reader sees it: the text value of a JSON file, or the text of a Markdown, text, HTML, XHTML, DOCX or PDF file without what the loader finds a reader cannot see. It cannot see layout, placement, prominence, images, audio or video, so wording shown only in an image, a banner or a voice-over is not read.
  • Looks for a label in English or German in the title or the first 40 words: crypto-asset marketing communication, marketing communication, advertisement, advertising, sponsored, sponsored content, paid promotion, paid partnership, promotional material, content or communication, Kryptowert-Marketingmitteilung, Marketingmitteilung, Werbemitteilung, Werbeanzeige, Werbung, Anzeige, gesponsert, bezahlte Partnerschaft. Whether the communication is clearly identifiable as such depends on its presentation, which the check cannot see. The title of a file other than JSON is its file name.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • The marketing communication checks have not yet been run against a real marketing communication.
