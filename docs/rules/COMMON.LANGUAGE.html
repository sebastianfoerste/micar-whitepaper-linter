How the status is set

  • Missing: section language is absent or empty, or holds only placeholder text such as TBD or TBC.
  • Review: the section names none of the 24 official languages of the EU.
  • Pass: the section names at least one of them by its English or German name, however short the section: Bulgarian (Bulgarisch), Croatian (Kroatisch), Czech (Tschechisch), Danish (Dänisch), Dutch (Niederländisch), English (Englisch), Estonian (Estnisch), Finnish (Finnisch), French (Französisch), German (Deutsch), Greek (Griechisch), Hungarian (Ungarisch), Irish (Irisch), Italian (Italienisch), Latvian (Lettisch), Lithuanian (Litauisch), Maltese (Maltesisch), Polish (Polnisch), Portuguese (Portugiesisch), Romanian (Rumänisch), Slovak (Slowakisch), Slovenian (Slowenisch), Spanish (Spanisch), Swedish (Schwedisch). Slovene counts as well. English names count as written or in capitals. German names count in any case, also inflected or with -sprachig: in deutscher Sprache, englischsprachig. A pass means a language name was found, nothing more.

Limitations

  • Reads one section key, language. Text elsewhere in the draft is not considered.
  • Looks for a language name only. The cited paragraphs require the white paper to be drawn up in an official language of the home Member State or in a language customary in the sphere of international finance, and likewise for a host Member State where the crypto-asset is also offered. They do not require the white paper to name its language; since 23 December 2025 the template of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2984 asks for it (field F.12 of Table 2, B.14 of Table 3, B.11 of Table 4). The check cannot tell which language the white paper is drawn up in, or whether that language meets the requirement. Language codes such as EN and names in other languages, such as français, do not count.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
