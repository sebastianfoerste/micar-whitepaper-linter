How the status is set

  • Missing: section not_approved_statement is absent or empty, or holds only placeholder text such as TBD or TBC.
  • Review: the draft is German, because the linter does not check the German wording yet; the section holds only a yes or no value; a listed English term does not appear; or “of the crypto-asset is solely responsible” appears without “offeror”, “person seeking admission to trading” or “operator of the trading platform” directly before it, as where the statement names the issuer or puts the offeror's name in between (“The offeror, Example AG, of the crypto-asset”).
  • Pass: an English draft holds both listed terms, and somewhere in the section “offeror”, “person seeking admission to trading” or “operator of the trading platform” comes directly before “of the crypto-asset is solely responsible”. Another person named elsewhere in the section is not checked. Matching ignores case and reads any run of spaces and line breaks as one space. A compound broken after its hyphen at a line end keeps the hyphen, so “crypto-” and “asset” on two lines match “crypto-asset”; a word split only for the line break (“Mem-”, “ber”) does not match and goes to review. Placement on the first page is not checked.

Limitations

  • Reads one section key, not_approved_statement. Text elsewhere in the draft is not considered.
  • Matches the English wording only. It cannot see whether the statement is on the first page, and it does not check that the person it names is the one responsible for the white paper.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • PDF, DOCX, Markdown and heading-based XHTML drafts fill the section from a heading such as “Statement in accordance with Article 6(3)”, the label of field 02 of Table 2 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2984, where the label is set as one (a Markdown “#” heading, an XHTML h1 to h6 or bold label, a DOCX heading or bold or capitals label, a PDF heading or field line), or, without one, from a line or paragraph that opens with the statement's first words. Numbered-field XHTML fills it from field 02. Table cells are not read in DOCX, nor in heading-based XHTML where the text sits directly in a td or th element rather than in a p, div, li or bold element inside it, so a statement kept only there is reported missing.
  • A German draft never passes: the linter does not check the German wording yet. The German Official Journal and template wording is recorded in docs/research/2026-10-31-oj-verification.md for a later check.
