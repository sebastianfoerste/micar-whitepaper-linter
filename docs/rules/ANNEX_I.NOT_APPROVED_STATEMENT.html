How the status is set

  • Missing: section not_approved_statement is absent or empty, holds only placeholder text such as TBD or TBC, or opens with the starter template's REPLACE: note.
  • Review: the section holds only a yes or no value; a listed term of the draft's language does not appear (the English terms for an English draft, the German terms for a German one); or the person is not the one the statement names: in an English draft, “of the crypto-asset is solely responsible” appears without “offeror”, “person seeking admission to trading” or “operator of the trading platform” directly before it, as where the statement names the issuer or puts the offeror's name in between (“The offeror, Example AG, of the crypto-asset”); in a German draft, “trägt die alleinige Verantwortung” appears without “Anbieter des Kryptowerts”, “Person, die die Zulassung des Kryptowerts zum Handel beantragt,” or “Betreiber der Handelsplattform des Kryptowerts” directly before it.
  • Pass: the draft holds both listed terms of its language, and somewhere in the section “offeror”, “person seeking admission to trading” or “operator of the trading platform” comes directly before “of the crypto-asset is solely responsible” (in German, “Anbieter des Kryptowerts”, “Person, die die Zulassung des Kryptowerts zum Handel beantragt,” or “Betreiber der Handelsplattform des Kryptowerts” before “trägt die alleinige Verantwortung”). Another person named elsewhere in the section is not checked. Matching ignores case and reads any run of spaces and line breaks as one space. A compound broken after its hyphen at a line end keeps the hyphen, so “crypto-” and “asset” on two lines match “crypto-asset”; a word split only for the line break (“Mem-”, “ber”) does not match and goes to review. Placement on the first page is not checked.

Legal basis

Art. 6(3) MiCAR

  • https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng

Checked on 2026-11-10 on EUR-Lex against the Official Journal text in English and German (OJ L 150, 9.6.2023) and against field 02 of Table 2 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2984 in English and German, which prints the responsibility sentence for each person. The only amending act EUR-Lex lists, Regulation (EU) 2023/2869, adds Art. 110a; no English or German corrigendum changes Art. 6(3), and 2024/2984 has no amendment or corrigendum.

Limitations

  • Reads one section key, not_approved_statement. Text elsewhere in the draft is not considered.
  • Matches the English and German wording only. The draft's language decides which, so a statement in the other of the two, or in a third language, goes to review. It cannot see whether the statement is on the first page, and it does not check that the person it names is the one responsible for the white paper.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • PDF, DOCX, Markdown and heading-based XHTML drafts fill the section from a heading such as “Statement in accordance with Article 6(3)” or “Erklärung gemäß Artikel 6 Absatz 3”, the English and German labels of field 02 of Table 2 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2984, where the label is set as one (a Markdown “#” heading, an XHTML h1 to h6 or bold label, a DOCX heading or bold or capitals label, a PDF heading or field line), or, without one, from a line or paragraph that opens with the statement's first words in English or German, also where they wrap onto the next lines. Text after the statement in its last line or paragraph, such as the management body's statement written in the same paragraph, stays in this section and is missing from its own. Numbered-field XHTML fills it from field 02. Table cells are not read in DOCX, nor in heading-based XHTML where the text sits directly in a td or th element rather than in a p, div, li or bold element inside it, so a statement kept only there is reported missing.
  • A German statement passes only with a responsibility sentence that field 02 of Table 2 prints. Art. 6 Abs. 3 UAbs. 2 MiCAR names the “Person, die die Zulassung zum Handel beantragt” but prints no German sentence for it, so a sentence in those words, without “des Kryptowerts”, goes to review, as does any other German wording.
