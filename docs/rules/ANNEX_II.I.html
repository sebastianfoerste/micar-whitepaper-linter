How the status is set

  • Missing: section redemption is absent or empty, or holds only placeholder text such as TBD or TBC.
  • Review: the section has fewer than 100 words, or a listed term does not appear (case-insensitive substring match, with any run of spaces and line breaks read as one space).
  • Review also follows when an English section contains neither “market value of the (referenced) assets” nor “by delivering” or “by delivery of” (the or those) (referenced) assets, the wording of the two bases of Art. 39(2) MiCAR, or mentions redemption at par, the e-money token rule (Art. 49(4) MiCAR). The phrases are matched as written: negation is not detected, and “delivery of the referenced assets” without “by” does not count. A German section needs “Rücktausch”, “Rücknahme” or “Rückzahlung”, and “Marktwert”: a German policy that redeems only by delivery goes to review, because the German wording of that alternative has not been checked.
  • Pass: none of the above. A pass means the check matched, nothing more.

Limitations

  • Reads one section key, redemption. Text elsewhere in the draft is not considered.
  • Matches words and counts length. It does not test accuracy, consistency with other sections or the point-level items of the cited Part.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • The Annex II rules have not yet been run against a real ART white paper.
  • The redemption-basis phrases are matched as written, so a negated one such as “never redeems by delivering the referenced assets” still counts.
  • “Rücktausch” and “Marktwert” follow reproductions of the German Art. 39 MiCAR in web search results and have not been checked against the German Official Journal; “Rücknahme” and “Rückzahlung” still count.
