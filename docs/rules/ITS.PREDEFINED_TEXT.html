How the status is set

  • Missing: in a numbered-field XHTML draft, none of fields 02 to 07 of Table 2, I.02 to I.04 and I.06 of Table 3 and I.02 to I.05 and I.07 of Table 4 (and field 05 of Table 2) holds an answer other than a placeholder.
  • Review: in a numbered-field draft, one of those fields is empty, absent, holds only a placeholder or is marked not applicable; a sentence of its predefined text is found neither in the template's English nor in its German wording; or a field whose form column has no free text holds more than 20 letters and digits besides the text and a heading such as “Warning” or “Regarding offerors”. Field 05 of Table 2 must be false (falsch) or hold the utility-token statement of Art. 6(5), point (d), MiCAR, with or without true; true alone, N/A or other text goes to review, and so does false where field D.6 or G.6 is true (utility token classification). The finding names each field and quotes the start of the template sentence it misses, never the draft.
  • Not run: in a draft without field numbers (JSON, PDF, DOCX, Markdown, heading-based XHTML) the check does not run. The report has no finding for this rule, neither open nor passed; one note names how many field-level checks did not run.
  • Pass: every field holds its text. Words are compared without case, punctuation, quotation marks, hyphens and spacing, with ä, ö, ü and ß read as ae, oe, ue and ss. A draft may leave out the full title of a cited act (“of the European Parliament and of the Council”), write “this” or “the” before “crypto-asset white paper”, add or leave out “that” before “any such offer”, name the token as crypto-asset, asset-referenced token or e-money token in the summary warning, and use either German wording of the summary warning. Field 02 of Table 2 counts in each of its three wordings (offeror, person seeking admission to trading, operator of the trading platform). Fields I.06 of Table 3 and I.07 of Table 4 may go on in free text after the predefined sentence.

Limitations

  • Reads the fields of a numbered-field XHTML draft by their numbers; text elsewhere is not considered. It does not run on a draft without field numbers.
  • Compares the English and the German wording only; a white paper in another official language goes to review.
  • Table 4 field I.03 leaves out “complete” (“vollständig”), which Art. 51(5) MiCAR has the management body confirm; the check accepts the statement with or without the word. Table 3 field I.02 misspells “asset-refenced token” in its second sentence; both spellings pass.
  • Up to 20 letters and digits besides the text pass, to allow a label that the loader could not split off; a short addition may therefore go unnoticed. It cannot see whether the statements stand on the first page, and it cannot tell which wording of field 02 fits the person who drew up the white paper.
  • Does not check the wording of the reimbursement notice (field E.15 of Table 2, C.10 of Table 3), which the template also predefines, nor field 09 of Table 2, which applies only where field 05 is true; ANNEX_I.E.P7, ANNEX_II.C.P5 and ANNEX_I.G.P4 check that these fields are filled.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • The template checks have not yet been run against a real notified white paper; for Tables 3 and 4 they follow the Official Journal text only.
