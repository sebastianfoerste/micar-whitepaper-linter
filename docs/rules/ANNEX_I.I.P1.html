How the status is set

  • Missing: in a numbered-field XHTML draft, every field below that applies is empty, absent or holds only a placeholder such as TBD; in any other draft, section risks is absent or empty, or holds only a placeholder.
  • Review: in a numbered-field draft, a field that applies is empty, absent or holds only a placeholder; a field that applies is marked not applicable (N/A, none, a dash) where the template does not ask for it “where applicable” or “if any”; or no field of an alternative holds an answer. A field that applies only under a condition is checked as applying when the draft does not show the condition (a flag that is neither true nor false, a code field without a template code). The finding names each field by its number and, in English, the template's name. In any other draft: always, with a note that the point check needs the numbered-field layout.
  • Pass: every field that applies holds an answer. A field that does not apply under its condition is not reported; the finding names the condition. An LEI counts as given only with valid check digits, a DTI as nine letters or digits; true and false count also as richtig and falsch, yes and no, ja and nein.

Limitations

  • Reads the fields of a numbered-field XHTML draft by their numbers, field I.1 of Table 2 and the fields their conditions name; text elsewhere is not considered. In a draft without field numbers it reads section risks only to tell whether it is absent; finding the point's items in free text is not built.
  • Checks whether each field holds an answer, not what it says: an answer to another question passes. ITS.FIELD_FORMATS and ITS.CODE_LISTS check the formats and codes of the typed fields.
  • Does not check a field's label, so a draft that numbers its fields otherwise is read in the wrong fields.
  • The point rules have not yet been run against a real notified white paper.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
