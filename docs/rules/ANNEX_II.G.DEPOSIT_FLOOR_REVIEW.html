ANNEX_II.G.DEPOSIT_FLOOR_REVIEW

Candidate deposit floor for the reserve (30 % under Art. 36(4)(d); 60 % under Art. 45(7)(b) for significant ARTs, or for non-significant ARTs via Arts. 35(4) and 45(3) MiCAR) - lawyer review required

How the status is set

  • Review: a characterisation flag (references_official_currency, art_significant, article_45_7b_required_by_authority) is missing or not a boolean; the draft states the token references no official currency; or a percentage is found in the section.
  • Missing: the flags determine a candidate floor and the section states no percentage.
  • Pass: never. Draft flags are assertions pending lawyer confirmation, not trusted facts.

Limitations

  • Reads one section key, reserve_of_assets. Text elsewhere in the draft is not considered.
  • Reads draft metadata and percentages only. It does not test accuracy or decide whether a floor is met.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • The Annex II rules have not yet been run against a real ART white paper.
  • This rule never reports Pass.
