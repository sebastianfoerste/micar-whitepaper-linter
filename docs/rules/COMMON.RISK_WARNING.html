How the status is set

  • Missing: section risk_warning is absent or empty, or holds only placeholder text such as TBD or TBC.
  • Review: the section has fewer than 20 words, or a term listed for the draft's regime does not appear (case-insensitive substring match, with any run of spaces and line breaks read as one space).
  • Pass: none of the above. A pass means the check matched, nothing more.

Art. 6(5) and Art. 19(4) MiCAR require a statement that the crypto-asset may lose its value in part or in full, may not always be transferable and may not be liquid, and that investor compensation and deposit guarantee schemes do not cover it; the listed terms come from that wording. Art. 51(4) asks an e-money token white paper only for the two scheme warnings, so EMT drafts are checked for those. The utility-token statement of Art. 6(5)(d) applies to utility tokens only and is not checked.

Limitations

  • Reads one section key, risk_warning. Text elsewhere in the draft is not considered.
  • Matches words and counts length. It does not test accuracy, consistency with other sections or the point-level items of the cited Part.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
