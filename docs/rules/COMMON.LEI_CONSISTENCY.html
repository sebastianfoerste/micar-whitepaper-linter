How the status is set

  • Missing: no section issuer, offeror or trading_platform_operator has text other than a placeholder.
  • Review: a party section has text but no party section carries an LEI with valid check digits, since point 5 allows another identifier in place of the LEI; a valid LEI in another section appears in no party section; or the issuer section names more than one valid LEI.
  • Pass: none of the above. Codes with invalid check digits are reported by COMMON.LEI_CHECK_DIGITS, not here.

Legal basis

Annex I, Part A, point 5, Part B, point 5, Part C, point 5, Annex II, Part A, point 5, Annex III, Part A, point 5 MiCAR

  • https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng

Checked on 2026-10-27 against the Official Journal text in English and German: point 5 of Annex I Parts A, B and C and of Annex II and III Part A names the legal entity identifier or another identifier required pursuant to applicable national law, and Art. 2(1)(b) and (c) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2984 ask for the LEI where available.

Limitations

  • Reads every section of the draft; findings are anchored to the issuer section.
  • Works offline on the characters of each code. It does not confirm that an LEI is registered, current or held by the entity the draft names.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • Does not recognise the other identifiers that point 5 allows in place of the LEI, so a party section without an LEI goes to review.
