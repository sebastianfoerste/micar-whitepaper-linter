How the status is set

  • Missing: no section issuer, offeror or trading_platform_operator carries an LEI with valid check digits.
  • Review: a valid LEI in another section appears in no party section, or the issuer section names more than one valid LEI.
  • Pass: none of the above. Codes with invalid check digits are reported by COMMON.LEI_CHECK_DIGITS, not here.

Limitations

  • Reads every section of the draft; findings are anchored to the issuer section.
  • Works offline on the characters of each code. It does not confirm that an LEI is registered, current or held by the entity the draft names.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • The citation is the Part of each Annex that the party rules already cite. The point that names the LEI has not been verified against the Official Journal; treat the legal basis as pending owner review.
