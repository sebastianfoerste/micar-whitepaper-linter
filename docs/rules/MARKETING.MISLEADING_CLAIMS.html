How the status is set

  • Missing: the text is empty or holds only placeholders such as TBD.
  • Review: the text holds one of the listed claims: a guaranteed return, no risk, a rising price or an approval by an authority. The finding names each claim, never the text.
  • Pass: none of the listed claims was found.

Limitations

  • Reads the whole text of the marketing communication as a reader sees it: the text value of a JSON file, or the text of a Markdown, text, HTML, XHTML, DOCX or PDF file without what the loader finds a reader cannot see. It cannot see layout, placement, prominence, images, audio or video, so wording shown only in an image, a banner or a voice-over is not read.
  • Finds the listed claims only, in the English and German wordings shown above, in a draft of either language. Whether the information is fair, clear and not misleading depends on all of it and on the white paper, so a pass means only that none of the listed claims was found. A negation right before a claim, such as "no guaranteed returns" or "not risk-free", does not count; one further back in the sentence is not recognised, so "No investment is risk-free" goes to review.
  • Status and severity are fixed by the rule. A draft cannot waive or downgrade them.
  • The marketing communication checks have not yet been run against a real marketing communication.
